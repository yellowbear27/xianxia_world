# Bei Ming (North Clan)

## Archetype
- Daoist hermits
- Arctic tribes
- Himalayan mystics

## Identity
Reclusive ascetics who view every daily act, including sweeping, carrying water, and enduring climate, as sacred cultivation.

## Core Identity
- Divine Beast: Xuan Wu (Lord of all tortoise and turtles)
- Peril Beast: Taotie
- Elements: Earth and Water

## Civilizational Character
The North is a culture of guardianship, burden, pressure, endurance, containment, and sacred routine.

## Philosophy & Religion
- Singular devotion to cultivation
- Loyalty is to the path itself
- Every daily act is cultivation
- Strong “sweeper monk” logic
- Deep ascetic orientation

## Society & Culture
- Decentralized leadership
- Schools may resemble:
  - forest monks
  - temple monks
  - Tibetan-style monks
- Everyone is a cultivator
- Outsiders may be welcome if genuinely called to the path
- Little to no propagandistic social machinery

## Economy & Infrastructure

Resources include:
- Yin Metal
- Glacial Jade
- Kaiju parts

Key infrastructure:
- Monasteries
- Hot springs
- Harsh settlements
- Defensive sacred geography

They rely on trade while facing:
- extreme environment
- food scarcity
- trade dependence
- frequent kaiju-scale threats

## Control & Psychology
- No formal mass-control system
- Membership is effectively voluntary
- Incentive = spiritual and martial progress
- Propaganda is absent or minimal

## Weak Points
- Extreme climate
- Resource scarcity
- Isolation
- Kaiju attacks
- Temptation of forbidden shortcuts

## Legacy
They are remembered as:
- sages
- hidden masters
- guardians of the impossible
- people who intervene only for existential-level threats

## Historical Burden
They have historically contained:
- ancient evils
- frozen horrors
- civilizational-scale threats

A notable example:
- Chi You’s frozen head

## Cultural Resonances
- Daoist hermits / ancient mystics
- Aboriginal / Indigenous Arctic cultures
- historical Norse settlers (Iceland / Greenland)
- Highland clans (Scotland / Caucasus)
- lost city / hidden kingdom archetype

## Martial Paths

### Foundational Art
**真武玄经 / 真武玄功**
- Infinite mass / density accumulation
- SSS-tier potential
- Glacially slow progression

### Dark Art
**吞天道**
- Heaven-devouring path
- Absolute consumption
- “Devour Heaven, World, Self… Xuan & Kun”

## Weapon Class

- Unarmed combat

- Staffs
- Hammers

- Rulers
- Unarmed methods

- 真武玄功 paradox: almost infinite slow progression
- ancient masters
- training in ice pools / lava pools
- only intervene for kaiju-scale threats
