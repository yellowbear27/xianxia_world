# Xi Yue (West Clan)

## Archetype
- Spartan
- Prussian
- Mongol bureaucracy

## Identity
Semi-nomadic survivors following the dual path of:

**煞之道，仁之路**
(the Way of Slaughter and the Path of Compassion)

## Core Identity
- Divine Beast: White Tiger
- Peril Beast: Qiongqi
- Elements: Metal and Wind

## Civilizational Character
A semi-nomadic warrior society descended from a raider phenotype, feared as mercenaries and survivors.

## Spiritual / Philosophical Framework
The White Tiger gave them little directly.

Power must be earned by oneself.

This becomes a civilizational ethic rather than a slogan.

The dark path was originally connected to Qiongqi.

Later, a mercenary leader fused slaughter with compassion to create a dual path identity.

## Historical Turning Point
After nearly self-exterminating, the clan was unified through a synthesis:
- 煞之道 (path of killing / slaughter)
- 仁之路 (path of compassion / humane principle)

This became their durable identity.

## Cultivation Outlook
Power is gained both through:
- killing
- restraint
- mercy
- understanding life and death

## Martial Philosophy
Influenced by life-and-death sword concepts:
- killing sword
- life-giving sword

## Culture & Arts
