# Nan Yan (South Clan)

## Archetype
- Tang Dynasty
- Sasanian Persia
- Meiji Japan

## Identity
A hyper-competitive, industrial, danger-forged society where geological violence and engineered wildlife shape the population into professionals of survival and warfare.

## Core Identity
- Divine Beast: Zhuque (Lord of Birds)
- Peril Beast: Taowu
- Elements: Fire and Light

## Geography & Environment
The South is geologically active:
- volcanoes
- earthquakes
- fertile land
- engineered wildlife defenses
- zones of persistent danger

Danger is not an anomaly. It is a civilizational input.

## Population & Culture
- Massive population
- Survival and competence are cultural virtues
- Martial training is economically sustainable
- Constant risk is normalized

## Warrior System
A professional, economically sustained force of millions can exist because:
- danger constantly sharpens the population
- guilds and houses organize opportunity
- mercenary work reinforces practical skill
- internal competition produces constant refinement

## Economy & Guild Structure
- Guilds and houses manage warriors
- Economic and martial power are tightly linked
- Wealth funds training
- Training generates value
- The system reinforces itself

## Strategic Advantage
- Soft borders
- Mutant wildlife
- Environmental hazards
- Battle-readiness as a normal social outcome

## Cultural & Historical Resonances
- Sasanian Persian Empire
- Tang Dynasty China
- Early Islamic Caliphates
- Imperial Japan (Meiji Era)

## Martial Paths

### Foundational Art
**赤羽神功** (Red Feather Divine Art / Chìyǔ Shéngōng)
- Rapid spiritual combustion
- Extreme speed / output

### Dark Art
**坤元大法** (Great Earth-Origin Method / Kūnyuán Dàfǎ)
- Fertility manipulation
- Decay logic
- Reproductive / generative distortion
