# THE CULTIVATION PHYSICS CURRICULUM (REVISED)

A logical progression from mathematical foundations to the implementation of a tensor-based Qi field engine.

---

## PHASE ZERO: THE LOGICAL FOUNDATION
*Goal: Master the language of logic and rigorous proof.*

1. **How to Prove It** (Daniel J. Velleman) — Learn to construct and deconstruct logical arguments.
2. **The Princeton Companion to Mathematics** (Timothy Gowers) — Overview of the mathematical landscape.

## PHASE ONE: CONTINUOUS CALCULUS & ANALYSIS
*Goal: Understand the change and accumulation of fields.*

1. **Calculus** (Michael Spivak) — Rigorous single-variable foundation.
2. **Calculus, Vol. II** (Tom Apostol) — Transition to multivariable and linear algebra.
3. **Principles of Mathematical Analysis** (Walter Rudin) — The "Baby Rudin" for deep theoretical maturity.

## PHASE TWO: LINEAR ALGEBRA & TENSORS
*Goal: Define the "Qi Cube" and its internal operations.*

1. **Introduction to Linear Algebra** (Gilbert Strang) — Computational and geometric intuition.
2. **Linear Algebra Done Right** (Sheldon Axler) — Abstract vector spaces (crucial for "hand size" logic).
3. **A Student's Guide to Vectors and Tensors** (Daniel Fleisch) — Initial entry into tensor notation.
4. **Geometrical Methods of Mathematical Physics** (Bernard Schutz) — Differential forms and tensors.

## PHASE THREE: DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS & DYNAMICS
*Goal: Model the flow of Qi and the Restoring Force (天道).*

1. **Elementary Differential Equations** (Boyce & DiPrima) — Solving standard ODEs.
2. **Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos** (Steven Strogatz) — Understanding instability and "Tribulation" triggers.
3. **Partial Differential Equations** (Walter Strauss) — Wave equations and field dynamics (□Q = J).

## PHASE FOUR: CLASSICAL & FIELD MECHANICS
*Goal: Quantify energy, force, and field interactions.*

1. **Classical Mechanics** (John R. Taylor) — Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics.
2. **Introduction to Electrodynamics** (David J. Griffiths) — The gold standard for understanding physical fields.
3. **The Classical Theory of Fields** (Landau & Lifshitz) — Relativistic field theory and tensors.

## PHASE FIVE: COMPUTATIONAL PHYSICS & ENGINE DESIGN
*Goal: Translate calculus into code (The Engine).*

1. **Computational Physics** (Mark Newman) — Numerical integration, ODE/PDE solvers in Python.
2. **Numerical Recipes** (Press et al.) — The "Bible" of numerical algorithms.

## PHASE SIX: ADVANCED PHENOMENA (SPECIALIZATION)
*Goal: Model specific Cultivator types (Lightning, Magnetism, Cold).*

1. **Introduction to Quantum Mechanics** (David J. Griffiths) — For "nascent soul" scale interactions.
2. **Thermal Physics** (Daniel V. Schroeder) — Thermodynamics of Qi absorption/emission.
3. **Introduction to Superconductivity** (Michael Tinkham) — For specialized Meissner effect techniques.

## PHASE SEVEN: THE GRAND SYNTHESIS
*Goal: Build the final simulator.*

1. **The I Ching** (Wilhelm translation) — Final mapping of the 64-hexagram state space.
2. **Implementation** — Full Python engine using NumPy and SciPy.

---
*Note: This curriculum is linear. Each phase provides the tools required for the next. Skipping phases will lead to logical gaps.*
