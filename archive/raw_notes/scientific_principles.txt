PART ONE: THE WORLD BUILDING ENGINE


A complete description of the project you intend to build.


---


1. Core Concept


A mathematically rigorous, physics-grounded cultivation system where:


· Qi is a fundamental tensor field permeating reality
· Cultivation is the art of perceiving, manipulating, and resonating with this field
· The I Ching (64 hexagrams) forms the discrete state space of cultivation
· The Five Elements govern the dynamic relationships between states
· Combat is the interaction of cultivators through field perturbations
· The Universe maintains equilibrium through a restoring force that corrects extremes


---


2. The Nature of Qi


Property Description
Qi is a field Not particles. Not marbles. A continuous presence everywhere.
Qi is a tensor field At minimum rank-2 (matrix at each point). Probably rank-3 for full richness.
Qi has an equilibrium state Q₀ — the natural, balanced state of Heaven and Earth.
Qi obeys field equations □Q = -κ(Q - Q₀) + J (wave equation with restoring force and sources).
Qi couples to physics Qi sources generate electromagnetic effects, thermal effects, gravitational effects through coupling tensors.


---


3. The 4×4×4 Cube (Cultivation Space)


The 64 hexagrams of the I Ching are arranged as a 3-dimensional cube of size 4×4×4.


Dimension Represents Coordinate Values
x-axis First pair of lines (positions 1-2) — Earth foundation 0,1,2,3 (four states of a line pair)
y-axis Second pair of lines (positions 3-4) — Human transformation 0,1,2,3
z-axis Third pair of lines (positions 5-6) — Heaven transcendence 0,1,2,3


Every hexagram is a point (i, j, k) in this cube. Cultivation is movement through this space.


---


4. The Five Elements in the Cube


Every hexagram has:


Elemental Layer Source Purpose
Palace Element Lower trigram (or palace system) Fixed elemental nature
Upper Trigram Element Upper trigram Outer expression
Lower Trigram Element Lower trigram Inner nature
Inner/Outer Relationship Generating (生) or Overcoming (克) cycle Dynamic tension within the hexagram


Movement through the cube follows elemental logic:


· Moving to a generating element (Wood → Fire) is smooth, nourishing
· Moving to an overcoming element (Wood → Earth) is difficult, tribulation-filled
· Moving to the same element is strengthening

5. Cultivation Realms as Tensor Operations


Realm Access Tensor Operation
Qi Condensation Perceive total Qi Read trace of tensor (scalar)
Foundation Sense elemental directions Read diagonal components
Core Formation Manipulate own element Write to one diagonal component
Nascent Soul Perceive elemental interactions Read off-diagonal components
Divine Transformation Convert between elements Write off-diagonal components
Immortal Create new elemental relationships Full tensor manipulation


At each realm, the cultivator's "hand size" (number of hexagrams they can actively hold) increases:


· Qi Condensation: 4 hexagrams
· Foundation: 8 hexagrams
· Core Formation: 16 hexagrams
· Nascent Soul: 32 hexagrams
· Divine Transformation: 64 hexagrams (full cube access)
· Immortal: Beyond the cube — creation of new hexagrams


---


6. The King Wen Sequence


The traditional order of hexagrams is understood as a projection of the 4×4×4 cube onto one dimension.


· King Wen is the appearance of fate — how Heaven shows the path
· The cube is the reality of cultivation — how the cultivator moves
· Every cultivator is born at a specific hexagram (their fate, their starting point)
· Their journey is a path through the cube
· The path, projected onto King Wen, appears as their destiny


---


7. The Restoring Force (天道)


The universe maintains balance. The field equation includes:


-κ(Q - Q₀)


Where:


· Q = current field value
· Q₀ = equilibrium field value
· κ = coupling constant (strength of Heaven's correction)


If a cultivator deviates too far from equilibrium — too lucky, too powerful, too extreme in any direction — the field corrects.


· Extreme good fortune triggers balancing misfortune
· Extreme power triggers tribulation
· Extreme cold triggers fire from nowhere


This is not punishment. This is field dynamics. The universe seeks balance.

Attribute Value

Attribute Value
Birth Hexagram Likely 29 (坎 — Water) or 39 (蹇 — Obstruction)
Element Water
Technique Absorbs heat, creates cold domains, uses superconducting sword

9. Combat Physics


Interaction Physics Equation
Lightning bolt Dielectric breakdown, plasma channel V_breakdown ~ 3MV/m, I ~ 30kA
Magnetic deflection Lorentz force F = q(v × B), or for currents: F = I × L × B
Cold domain Heat absorption Q = m·c·ΔT
Superconducting sword Meissner effect B = 0 inside superconductor → no Lorentz force
Damage Thermal, electrical, mechanical Tissue damage thresholds, current lethality

10. The Final Goal


A simulation engine that:


· Takes two cultivator specifications (birth hexagram, realm, techniques)
· Calculates field interactions using real physics
· Determines outcome based on energy transfer, deflection, damage, and restoring force
· Outputs narrative description + numerical result
· Is extensible to new cultivator types, techniques, and environments
